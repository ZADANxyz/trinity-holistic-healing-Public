## ✨ Key Features

The official website is the digital home for The Trinity Holistic Healing practice, featuring:

*   **🌿 Integrative Services**: A clear presentation of the practice's holistic medicine and counseling services, including child therapy and whole-person care.
*   **👩‍⚕️ Practitioner Profiles**: Dedicated, SEO-friendly profile pages for each member of the care team, so clients can get to know their providers.
*   **📝 Articles & Insights**: A wellness blog with individual article pages and Substack-integrated content on holistic health topics.
*   **🔊 Calming Audio**: Built-in ambient audio controls to create a soothing, grounded browsing experience.
*   **🗂️ Rich Content Pages**: Flexible, dynamically generated pages for services, resources, and information.
*   **🔐 Private Admin Tools**: A protected admin area for the team to manage site content (not publicly accessible).
*   **🚀 SEO Optimized**: A full sitemap, structured metadata, and thoughtful internal linking for strong discoverability.
*   **📱 Fully Responsive**: A warm, calming, mobile-first design that works beautifully on any device.
